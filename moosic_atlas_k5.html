How to read the Atlas
  • K-Means is the operating model — ask for k clusters, every track is assigned, no outliers, scales cleanly.
  • Ward agglomerative is the conversation tool. The dendrogram goes on the wall when you debate splits with the music team.
  • DBSCAN is the quality filter. 931 noise points (17.8%) sit in no dense neighbourhood — editorial puzzles, not errors.
  • Silhouette scores: k=2: 0.333 → k=3: 0.271 → k=4: 0.276 → k=5: 0.267 → k=6: 0.248. Best interpretable balance at k=5.
  • The Atlas drafts; a person listens. Audio features cannot hear lyrics, language, era or context.
